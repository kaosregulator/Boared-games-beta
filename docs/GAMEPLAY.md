# Gameplay ledger

This is the running record for the room and the mini-games. A row stays here until the same steps have been run again after the change.

## Fixed in this pass

| Area | What was wrong | Root cause | Fix | How it was checked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room crouch | C and Ctrl crouched only while held. | `WalkController` treated those keys as a held button. The on-screen Duck button used a second flag, so the two could disagree. | C toggles one latch. Space stands. The Duck button uses the same latch. Ctrl is left alone so shortcuts still work. Hot Wheels and Army Men still use C for their cameras, because the walker ignores C while those modes own the keys. | `tests/room/controls.test.ts`. Browser: enter the room, press C, `data-crouch` stays `1` after the key is released, press C again and it returns to `0`. |
| Look settings | Sensitivity and invert were not settings. The menu had no device choice. | Look speed was a constant in the drag handler. | Desktop, Laptop, Tablet, and Phone presets set a look-speed multiplier and save it in `room-controls`. The slider and invert checkboxes in Room studio apply on the next drag. WASD is unchanged. | `tests/room/controls.test.ts` for the math and the save. Browser: the title reel shows the four presets and the pressed one is saved. |
| Hot Wheels steering | A stopped car, or a car scrubbing a wall, barely turned. | Steering grip was `speed / 0.22`, so it was zero at rest. A head-on bump holds speed near zero, so the nose never came off the wall. | Grip keeps a 0.45 floor, then rises to the old curve once the car is moving. High-speed easing is unchanged. No teleport. | `tests/room/carSteer.test.ts`. Before the change a half-second of full steer at rest left heading at 0, and 1.5 s pinned on the dresser turned only 0.07 rad. After the change both assertions pass. |
| Furby table | "Open the new ones at the table" did not open the table. | The engine bar was a wide empty box at the top of the screen, so on a phone or a narrow window the click landed on that box. The box pull also added a 50 ms frame cap, so a slow frame never finished the open. | The engine control is only as wide as its button. The table request is recorded before the put-down. The flight and the pull follow real time. | `tests/room/furbyTable.test.ts`. Browser at 390px: the open button is the element under the cursor, the click shows "Opening Furby", and the play actions appear after the pull. The card that then sat on the toys is the Furby play card row. |
| Clue cards | Suggestion cards stood up 50 ms apart, the note appeared in one paint, the card someone showed you was small, and Mrs. White's name was white on a white band. | `CardFan` delayed each card by `i * 0.05`. The table HUD printed the whole note at once. The shown card was 0.16 m away at scale 1. The name was drawn in white. | A fan waits 0.14 s between cards and each card rises over 0.62 s. The Clue note types on the clock, about 0.7 s for a line, and a late tick catches up. The shown card is closer and larger and stays until Continue. Faces keep the existing portraits, weapons, and floors, inside a coloured border, with the name in black on a cream plaque. The board camera is the same. | `tests/room/cluePace.test.ts`. Browser at 1280: the accuse fan shows six readable suspect cards under "Accuse: who did it?". A suggestion in the Study put the Study card up at scale 1.65, Continue was still there, and the note read "Professor Plum shows you the Study." The same card was readable at 390px, below the status card. This machine only painted every couple of seconds, so the letters in between were not sampled; `typedCount` checks 120 ms and 900 ms. |
| Army aim | A shot knocked down a soldier 16° off the barrel, and the streak bent over to them. A soldier straight ahead past the short streak did not fall. | The player shot picked the nearest body inside a 0.45 rad cone and 0.75 m, then flipped a coin. The streak was drawn to that body, or stopped at 0.5 m. | The player's shot follows the barrel line out to 1.15 m. A body has to sit within a little more than its own radius of that line. The streak runs from the muzzle to that body, or straight out on a miss, and stays up for 0.16 s. The other soldiers still use their cone. The cameras are unchanged. | `tests/room/armyAim.test.ts`: the old cone hits the 16° soldier and misses the one at 0.8 m; the barrel line does the opposite. Browser, waves held still: the soldiers at 0.3 m and 0.8 m on the line fell, the one 0.15 m off to the side did not, and the one at 1.3 m did not. |
| Army waves | The attack ended after nine tan soldiers. There was no way to keep going, and no Easy, Medium, or Hard. | `WAVES` was `[5, 7, 9]`, and clearing the third wave set the town to won. | Those three waves are still 5, then 7, then 9, with the same walk speed and aim. Easy, Medium, and Hard are on the opening and on the town-saved screen. They do not end: the next wave is larger, up to the 16 tan soldiers in the town, and Hard walks faster and shoots farther. | `tests/room/armyWaves.test.ts`. Browser: story wave 1 put 5 out and wave 3 put 9 out, and a fourth story wave did not start. Hard wave 1 put 8 out at a faster march; hard wave 8 put 16 out. The Medium button set the siege to medium and the panel read Medium. |
| Army tutorial | The first green soldier stayed on the parade and never explained the controls. | There was no lesson. The drill squad only walked its route. | That soldier walks toward the player until he is within 0.11 m, then faces them. The bubble stays hidden until then. Move waits for a step, aim waits for a turn, shoot waits for a shot, and then he goes back to the parade. Easy, Medium, and Hard skip the lesson. | `tests/room/armyTutorial.test.ts`. Browser, story boot: his distance went from 0.910 to 0.898 at speed 0.09 and the bubble stayed hidden. At 0.05 m the bubble read "W and S walk. A and D turn you." | Furby play card | At 390px the play card covered the Boom's ears and the top of the display box. | The card is pinned to the top right and becomes the width of a narrow screen. | Furby lights | The new Furby ignored the wall switch, and a line said on the bed never appeared. | Only the bed toy was told the lights were out. | Hot Wheels race line | In a race it was hard to stay on the plastic: the turn into the ramp, the boosters and the flight to the desk were missed from every camera. | The car was never glued to the track, and floor runs had no rails. A bump only reverses the steering once you are really backing up, and hands-off the nose glances along the wall. | `tests/room/carRace.test.ts`: corner escape, the plate, the Hot Wheels set's corner. A random-drive run (60 starts × 40 s, steering one way out when stuck) is never held more than about 5 s, and only in the room's corners. |
